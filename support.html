Common questions

I forgot my password. On the sign-in screen tap "I already have an account", then "Forgot password?". Open the email on the same phone.

My partner's invite code doesn't work. Codes last 24 hours and work once. Ask them to open the app for a fresh one.

I'm not getting notifications. Check Settings → Apps → Hugli → Notifications, then open Hugli once so it can re-register.

I want to stop sharing. Privacy tab: switch categories off, pause, or unlink. It takes effect immediately.

Something feels wrong or unsafe. Unlink straight away on the Privacy tab, use "Report" on a note, and email us. In an emergency call 000.

To delete your account, see Delete your account.
