Hugli

Little ways to look after each other.

Love is making sure they ate. Hugli is a private little app for two: share how your day is going with one tap, and send a hug, a nudge or a note when they need it.

Care, not control

One tap

"I ate", "+1 water", "good morning". No typing, no fuss.

Only when it matters

A gentle heads-up if they haven't had lunch, not a stream of updates.

Send a little love

Hugs, nudges, voice notes and little notes that wait at the top of their screen.

Made for distance

Sees the day on their clock, so "2 pm" means their 2 pm.

Your privacy, by design

Hugli only works between two people who have both chosen to link. You decide what your partner sees, you can pause sharing anytime, and either of you can unlink instantly.

No location. No ads. Nothing sold. Read the privacy policy.

Hugli is not a medical or emergency service.
