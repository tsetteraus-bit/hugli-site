Hugli

Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

The short version

Hugli helps two people who have both agreed to it look after each other: meals, water, sleep, mood, hugs and short voice notes. You choose what your partner can see, you can pause sharing at any time, and either of you can unlink instantly. We never collect your location, we never sell your information, and there are no ads.

Who we are

Hugli is operated by Saurabh Dhokwal (ABN 46 284 804 580) in Melbourne, Australia. Questions or requests: hello.hugliapp@gmail.com. Website: https://hugli.app.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (stored securely by our hosting provider; we never see your password).
  • Profile: the name you choose, your avatar (a Hugli critter, or a picture you upload) and your timezone.
  • Check-ins you make: meals, water, waking up, going to sleep, mood, "busy" status, hugs and nudges, with the time.
  • Your own check-ins, if you add any (for example "Medicine taken"): the words and icon you choose, whether you share them, and when you tap them. Hugli does not interpret them and never alerts anyone if you miss one.
  • Voice notes you record for your partner (up to 30 seconds each), and little notes you write (up to 140 characters), and whether your partner gave your note a heart.
  • If you report a note, a copy of it, who sent it and when, kept so we can review it.
  • Your sharing and notification settings. Your look (light or dark, colour) and reminder times stay on your phone.
  • Pairing details: your invite code and who you are linked with.
  • A notification token for your phone, so we can deliver notifications from your partner.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or health data from other apps, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

How it is used

Only to run Hugli for you and your partner: showing your partner what you choose to share, sending the notifications you have switched on (for example a hug, a nudge, or a missed-lunch alert), and keeping your account working. We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it.

Who can see it

  • Your linked partner sees only the categories you share, and nothing while sharing is paused (hugs, nudges, little notes, voice notes and "busy" still get through, because they are messages rather than tracking). They see your avatar, and the voice notes you send them.
  • Nobody else. Privacy rules are enforced by our database itself, not just by the app.
  • To run and improve Hugli, we (the operator) can see basic account details: your email address, when you signed up and were last active, whether you are linked, and counts of activity (for example, how many check-ins were made). We do not look at your check-ins, notes, voice notes or pictures, except a note that someone reports to us.
  • When you unlink, your partner immediately stops seeing your information and your voice notes.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run Hugli:

  • Supabase: hosts the database, sign-in and file storage, in Sydney, Australia.
  • Expo: delivers notifications to your phone and builds the app.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android) and Apple Push Notification service (iPhone): carry notifications to your device.

Notifications contain a short message such as "Sam sent you a hug". Some of these providers may process data outside Australia.

How long we keep it

  • Voice notes are deleted automatically after 7 days, or sooner if the person who received it deletes it, or the sender unsends it before it is played.
  • Little notes are deleted 7 days after they are dismissed or expire, or straight away if the sender removes them. Reported notes are kept until reviewed.
  • An old avatar picture is deleted when you change or remove it.
  • Everything else is kept while you have an account, and deleted when you delete your account.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: You tab (Privacy in older versions) → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, check-ins, avatar pictures, voice notes (sent and received), settings and notification tokens, and unlinks your partner. It cannot be undone. If you can no longer sign in, email hello.hugliapp@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete it for you.

Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles you can ask to access or correct your information, or complain about how it has been handled. Email hello.hugliapp@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied with our response you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Not a medical or emergency service

Hugli is a gentle way to care for each other. It is not a medical device, a health monitor or a safety service, and it is not watched by anyone. In an emergency, call 000 (in Australia) or your local emergency number.

Age

Hugli is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
